A share link carries the receipt in the part of the URL after the # — it is never sent to any server. Anyone who opens the link sees the verdict in their browser, no app, no account. Verification is free forever because a seal that only paying parties can check isn't a seal.

What this proves: the claims, issuer, subject and meta in the receipt are exactly what was sealed (Merkle root + receipt digest), and — for hmac mode with the right key, or ed25519 mode with the public key printed on the receipt — that the named issuer sealed them. detached mode proves integrity only, never who issued it. flagged receipts were exported unsigned because too many claims were unverified.
