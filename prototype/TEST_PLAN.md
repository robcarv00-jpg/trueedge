# The 5-bettor test — script and rules (written before any session runs)

The gate for everything after the prototype. No TrueEdge build, no app
build, until this loop has run at least once.

## Cohort

- 5 bettors first. Iterate. Then 10. Then 20. Never 20 at once.
- Skew MID-LEVEL: regular bettors who do not track CLV today. Sharp
  Twitter cappers already know their numbers and will say "I knew that" —
  that teaches nothing. The product is for people who don't know.
- Sources: r/sportsbook volunteers, Pikkit users with public journals,
  local bettors Robert knows. Their real journal (CSV export) is the entry
  ticket — no journal, no session.

## Session script (30 minutes, recorded with permission)

1. Hand them their own sealed Mirror report. Say nothing for 30 seconds.
   Watch where their eyes go first: the score, or the leaks. That order
   is data — it says what the product's headline actually is.
2. The only framing question: **"Which line in this report is wrong?"**
   Never "do you like it." Politeness is not signal.
3. For every line they challenge, write down their exact words and which
   line. A challenged line means one of two fixable things: a bug, or a
   calibration/presentation problem. Both are gold.
4. Close with: "If this updated every Sunday, would you open it?" — and
   record the pause before they answer, not just the answer.
5. Then the tier question (added 2026-10-25, after the two-tier lock):
   "If this were paid, which tier would you need — the one that shows
   your history, or the one that tells you what to do next?" Do NOT
   show prices. What this measures is cannibalization pressure: which
   feature set they reach for decides whether semis settle for Plus
   or need Pro. Five answers are not proof, but five-for-five reaching
   for Pro features is an early warning worth having before the
   paywall is built.

## Kill / iterate criteria (decided in advance, not after)

- PASS: at least 3 of 5 point at a specific line and say some version of
  "that's me" unprompted, and at least 3 of 5 say they'd open the Sunday
  update.
- ITERATE: challenges cluster on one line or one tier label — fix that
  line's math or wording and rerun with 5 fresh bettors.
- KILL/PIVOT: 3 or more of 5 say "I already knew all of this" or can't
  find themselves in the report at all. The diagnosis isn't landing;
  more features will not fix it.

## Rules

- Record every session (with consent). Notes from memory are not data.
- No explaining the rubric mid-session unless asked — if the report needs
  a narrator, the report is the bug.
- Rubric version is frozen during a cohort round. Mid-round rubric
  changes invalidate the round (receipts pin versions for this reason).
